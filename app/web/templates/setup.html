4 · Job sources

Company job boards, job platforms (Workable, Careerjet, Jooble), the Bundesagentur für Arbeit and academic boards are checked automatically. Job sites — which sites are connected in each country · Manage sources · companies.yaml (advanced)

5 · Job alert e-mails optional

LinkedIn, Indeed, StepStone, Bayt, GulfTalent and NaukriGulf don't allow automated searches — but their job alert e-mails work. Create alerts for your roles on those sites, sent to one mailbox, and connect that mailbox in .env (IMAP_HOST, IMAP_USER, IMAP_PASSWORD). Status: Operations.

6 · AI engine

Local models (private, free — needs an NVIDIA GPU with ≥ 12 GB) run in the ollama container; or set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY / OPENAI_API_KEY in .env. Current roles: Operations.

7 · Job platform API keys optional

Free keys bring in postings from aggregators that index the big boards (Bayt, GulfTalent, NaukriGulf, …): CAREERJET_API_KEY (careerjet.com/partners) and JOOBLE_API_KEY (jooble.org/api/about) in .env.

8 · Password and backups

Set APP_PASSWORD in .env before the app is reachable by anyone else. The database is copied to data/backups every day (newest 7 kept) — back up now.
